Trying to document some of the  restaurants I have tried and found worth sharing in Kodaikanal.

A fact that's common across  tourists locations in India is that its very hard to find restaurants that are loved by the locals of that place. 
More often we are tempted to have the food from the city centres or those which has high google ratings. I'm of the belief that the ratings  are manipulated and meant to cater to transitionary tourists who come for a short visit.

So this is my attempt to try to share my experience in the hope that it might help someone else.

## George's Gourmet Kitchen

We visited this place for the first time in May 2025.
We  tried Garlic Bread, Bagels, Croissants, Pizza and the specialty sodas.
We were fortunate to meet Mr. George who came to came to our table and sought for our feedback. I have never seen an owner reaching out so enthusiastically to have chat with us.  
If you have tastebuds like me, you'll not like the Orange marmalade that's given with the Bagels. That's too bitter. Go for Cream cheese instead.

### Tips
The timing of the restaurant  is from 11am to 4pm. 
Their Croissants are really good - but its only made in the weekends
They have wide varieties sauces, jams and cookies for sales.
We particularly loved their Sour-dough bread!
