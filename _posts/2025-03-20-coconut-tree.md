The coconut tree is often referred to as the "Tree of Life" due to its many uses and benefits. It's not only about coconut oil; the entire tree offers valuable resources.

- **Coconut Water:** A refreshing drink full of electrolytes.
- **Coconut Meat:** A versatile ingredient in cooking.
- **Coconut Leaves:** Used for thatching and weaving.
